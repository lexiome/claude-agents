# First users

**Rule:** don't launch; recruit people one by one and learn from each one. A launch comes after about 100 people use <your-app> on their own. Keep your channels and claim rules in one doc.

## Circles, closest first
1. **People who know the founder:** family and friends who manage someone's medicines, starting with your first 10 testers. Ask them to use it with made-up details now, and for honest feedback, not posts.
2. **Your community:** the groups where your users already talk (forums, chat groups, subreddits). Before asking for anything, help first by answering questions and sharing useful explainers.
3. **Strangers, one message at a time:** doctors, pharmacies and clinic staff who see families carrying paper files. Write each message for that person, never a template blast.

## Never
- Never message patients cold, and never ask for real records before the real-data gate.
- Never promise more than the app does. <your-app> organises records; it does not give advice.
- Never bulk-email the waitlist before double opt-in exists.

## Measure what people do
- The real gates are your kill metrics (for example week-1 and month-3 return).
- Track, for each person: invited, signed in, did the core action, came back in week 1. Each row is one person, not a guess.
- Every person who drops out is a lesson: either the wrong person, or the product needs work. Write it in `docs/planning/research/` via @user-researcher.

## Output
1. 10 named people for this week, from circles 1 and 2.
2. The one-line ask for each, in the user's own language.
3. What we will learn from them, and how we will see it.
4. This week's goal, for example "5 sign in, 3 come back".
